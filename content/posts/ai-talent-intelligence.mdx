[View project on GitHub](https://github.com/brownmd/AI_Talent_Intelligence)

---


## Scope & Limitations

This dataset tracks **public, behavioral AI activity** on GitHub—not self-reported skills.

**Important:** This does not capture all AI-literate activity on GitHub. Geographic analysis is limited to users with parseable location data.

**Coverage:**
- 5.81M monthly records (Jan 2021–Mar 2026)
- 2.58M unique users with observed AI activity
- 322,868 with country-level resolution
- 235,054 with city-level resolution

## Part III: Market Trends & Momentum

### Reading the Growth Signal

The clearest signal is cumulative expansion. US-located monthly users grew roughly 6x from 2021 to 2026:

- **January 2021:** 507 US users with verified location observed in that month
- **March 2023:** 2,922 US-located users observed in that month
- **January 2025:** 6,417 US-located users observed in that month
- **March 2026 (latest):** 3,133 US-located users observed in that month

## Implications

**What 2.58M verified users means:**

This is a large, behavior-based dataset of public AI engagement on GitHub—but not a complete census of all AI-literate workers.

**The signal:** 16,323 users (Jan 2021) → 502,049 (Mar 2023) → 1.74M (Feb 2025) → 2.58M (Mar 2026). AI literacy has gone mainstream.

**For talent acquisition teams:** The differentiator is no longer access to data, but synthesis of external market signals with internal hiring outcomes. Evidence-based capability planning beats guesswork every time.
