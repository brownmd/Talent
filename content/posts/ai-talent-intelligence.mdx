This report tracks verified public AI engagement on GitHub: the infrastructure-level contributors, the geographic hotspots, and the market momentum underlying talent acquisition strategy.

Based on 5.81M monthly activity records on GitHub of 2.58M unique users with resolved locations, this dataset reveals where AI capability is concentrating and accelerating.

**Project Details:** [AI Talent Intelligence](/projects/ai-talent-intelligence) · [GitHub](https://github.com/brownmd/AI_Talent_Intelligence)

## Scope & Limitations

This dataset tracks **public, behavioral AI activity** on GitHub—not self-reported skills or hiring pipeline data.

**Important coverage notes:**
- Does not capture all AI-literate activity on GitHub (incomplete due to private repos, archived accounts, bot filtering)
- Geographic analysis limited to 322,868 users (11%) with country-level location resolution and 235,054 (9%) with city-level resolution
- Scoring reflects observed contribution behavior, not skill assessment or project quality
- Monthly activity subject to data collection timing and GitHub API constraints
- Top performers may include bots that passed initial spam filtering, research accounts, and organizational accounts

See [full project documentation](https://github.com/brownmd/AI_Talent_Intelligence) for complete methodology.

## Implications

**What 2.58M verified users means:**

**For talent acquisition teams:** The differentiator is no longer access to data, but *synthesis* of external market signals with internal hiring outcomes. Evidence-based capability planning beats guesswork every time.

**Market concentration:** 10% of US AI-engaged users are in one metro (Bay Area). Another 10% are in the next top-5 metros. Geographic arbitrage and secondary-market talent sourcing are increasingly viable.

**Growth velocity:** 52.5% YoY user growth (2024-2025) and 60.4% score growth signal sustained demand and deepening expertise. The market is not cooling.

*Data sourced from GitHub public activity records, January 2021 - March 2026. AI activity scoring reflects engagement with AI/ML repositories weighted by contribution type. Geographic normalization applied to 92.4% of self-reported locations. All figures reflect post-quality-review production dataset after bot and spam account removal.*
