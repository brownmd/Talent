## Bio

I'm David, a Talent Acquisition professional, father of three boys, married to my high school sweetheart, and dog dad to Beo, our 75lb golden doodle.

I got into TA because I love building winning teams, scouting, performance and finding an edge on how to excel.

I have always been the kind of person who reads the back of baseball cards for hours, consuming the numbers and the story behind them. I'm a back of the baseball card kind of guy, and I love how numbers tell a story of the past and how they can predict future outcomes. Within this space of TA, I plan on exploring concepts within TA with numbers and creating insights into our industry.

This site is my professional passion journal, a place to experiment, create, and explore the art and science of Talent Acquisition. Talent intelligence, TA technology, and personal projects.

Thanks for visiting! :)
